# PinPad
A pocket-sized multitool and hacker badge designed for makers and tinkerers. Inspired by the sleek, minimalist aesthetic of the Kode Dot, PinPad packs dense hardware into a clean, compact vertical form factor.

✨ Key Features:
The Brain (ESP32-S3): A powerful dual-core microcontroller with built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, paired with 8 MB PSRAM and 32 MB Flash for smooth UI animations and custom apps.

Screen, Touchpad & Rear Buttons: The top section features a large rounded-square display, paired with a compact, wide capacitive touchpad right below it and two ergonomic rear-mounted physical buttons for quick actions. Together they form an intuitive control deck for gestures, navigation, and mini-games.

Motion & Spatial Awareness: An onboard 6-axis accelerometer and gyroscope (IMU) let PinPad react to every tilt, shake, and movement in real time.

Audio & Ambient Glow: A built-in I2S MEMS microphone unlocks audio-reactive visuals, sound monitoring, and voice commands, while a rear-facing RGB LED adds ambient underglow and acts as a customizable status indicator.

Tactile Haptics: A miniature vibration motor provides crisp haptic feedback for touch gestures, interactions, and alerts.

Expandable Storage: A side-mounted microSD card slot gives you gigabytes of space for logging sensor data, adding custom apps, and storing audio clips.

Modular 6-Pin Pogo Expansion & Charging: Rear-mounted pogo pins allow effortless snap-on connections to custom add-on boards, external sensors, or magnetic charging docks without messy cables.

Modern Power & Connectivity: Bottom-mounted USB Type-C with smart power management and accurate battery fuel-gauge monitoring for fast flashing, cool charging, and serial or HID communication with your PC.

More than just a dev board—PinPad is a pocket-sized canvas for creative coding, modular hardware, and interactive art.
